PerfCrew  ·  AI performance-engineering team

Cut your inference bill. Safely. And remember why.

Three AI agents work in one Band room: they benchmark cheaper ways to serve your model on a real GPU, pass a Snyk security gate, get the founder's OK, bill through Flexprice, and store every lesson in Cognee, so each audit is smarter than the last.

Band

02 · WHAT THE TEAM KNOWS
Cognee
Long-term memory, two knowledge graphs
